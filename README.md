## About

Data Scientist and AI Engineer with a Master's in Artificial Intelligence, working at the
line where research meets production. I build **language models from scratch in PyTorch**,
**agentic RAG systems** that survive contact with real users, and **anomaly detection on
non-stationary industrial time-series** — plus the pipelines, evaluation harnesses and
monitoring that keep them honest once they ship.

Four years turning raw data into decisions, from executive dashboards to machine learning
running in production.

**Currently** — finishing an M.Sc. in Artificial Intelligence at Brandenburgische Technische
Universität Cottbus, with a thesis on unsupervised anomaly detection for non-stationary
industrial multivariate time-series, researched at **Metso**.
